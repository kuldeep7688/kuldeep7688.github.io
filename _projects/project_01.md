## Description
A full-stack voice processing platform built with FastAPI and React, offering voice cloning, multilingual text-to-speech, and ASR transcription. Powered by Nvidia NIM speech models — Magpie TTS, Canary ASR, and Chatterbox — served through the Nvidia NIM API.
