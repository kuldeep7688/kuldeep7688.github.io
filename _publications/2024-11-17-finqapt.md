FinQAPT is an end-to-end pipeline that closes the gaps in real-world financial question answering, from identifying relevant documents to reasoning over structured and unstructured data using LLMs, achieving state-of-the-art results on FinQA. This work was awarded **Best Paper Runner-Up** at the 5th ACM International Conference on AI in Finance (ICAIF 2024).
