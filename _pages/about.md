Hello, and welcome to my website! I am Kuldeep Singh, a Senior Machine Learning Engineer with 7+ years of industry experience building and deploying NLP and LLM systems. I currently work at [Quantiphi](https://quantiphi.com/), on-site with client [AT&T](https://www.att.com/), where I focus on LLM post-training and evaluation. I fine-tune open-source models that replace GPT-4o and GPT-4.1 in production workflows, beating them by 5 to 10% on use-case metrics and saving roughly $800K/year. I also owned the integration of reinforcement learning (GRPO, PPO, GSPO) into AT&T's Finetuning-as-a-Service platform and architected an evaluation microservice (LLM-as-a-judge and code-based judges) now used by 10+ teams. My research and engineering interests center on **LLM post-training and reinforcement learning, evaluation methodology, Retrieval-Augmented Generation, and agentic systems**.

Before Quantiphi, I was an AI Engineer at [BYO](https://byo.digital/), where I built RAG agents with guardrails and reproduced TRACE continual learning results on Llama-3.1-8B, and before that an AI Consultant with the [Department of Family Medicine at Michigan State University](https://medicine.chm.msu.edu/), where I improved retrieval recall in a clinical RAG system by 22% using AWS Bedrock and OpenSearch. That MSU work grew out of my Master of Science in Data Science there (GPA 4.00/4.00), where I collaborated with Professors [Ling Wang](https://medicine.chm.msu.edu/directory/wang-l.html) and [Jiayu Zhou](https://jiayuzhou.github.io/) on LLM-based evaluation of clinical notes and Open Domain Question Answering, and completed a capstone under Professor [Mohammad Ghassemi](https://engineering.msu.edu/faculty/Mohammad-Ghassemi) on predicting funding flows in public and private markets. I hold a Bachelor's in Civil Engineering from [Indian Institute of Technology Roorkee](https://www.iitr.ac.in/).

During summer 2023, I was an AI Research Associate Intern at [JP Morgan & Chase](https://www.jpmorganchase.com/), working under [Simerjot Kaur](https://www.linkedin.com/in/simerjot-kaur-3429ab2a/) and [Charese Smiley](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=V2UiOeoAAAAJ&hl=en) on a financial question-answering system that reached SOTA results on FinQA. That work became my ["FinQAPT" paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.13959), which won **Best Paper Runner-Up at ACM ICAIF 2024**.

Earlier in my career I worked at [ConcertAI](https://www.concertai.com/), Bengaluru and [Innoplexus Consulting Pvt. Ltd.](https://www.innoplexus.com/), Pune. At ConcertAI, I built information extraction models for Electronic Health Records of cancer patients, using weak supervision (SkWeak, Snorkel) to scale training data. At Innoplexus, I built NER and relation classification models on biomedical data and helped construct a biomedical knowledge base for drug discovery pipelines.

For the full breakdown of roles, publications, and skills, see my [CV](/cv/).
